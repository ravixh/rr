Ravir Scott is an independent music artist, author, developer and filmmaker from Sonma, Begusarai, Bihar.

Self-made, with no label, no studio and no co-writers. He writes, composes, produces, films and codes everything himself — rap and hip-hop with a cinematic sound, and now the bamboo flute.

His album SANGAM (2026) is emotional hip-hop — a confluence of past, pain and purpose in one sound. His flute work, Flutes of Sanātana, says what hip-hop tells out loud: what it feels like. His film Lone Yet Loyal (2025) — director, writer, actor, composer, editor — is a solo production, listed on IMDb.

He has written five books, including two autobiographies, Turning Data Into Intelligence, Blog with Next.js, and The Ravir, a Hindi analysis of Machiavelli. He also builds things: Ravir Songs, a cinematic music player; Ravir Rank AI, for digital identity; Ravir Press, tech news; and ANONCHAT, encrypted anonymous chat.

His music is released on his own label, Ravir's Wire. You can write to him at hi@ravish.cyou, or visit his main site ravish.cyou.
